# Personal Automation Tools

I maintain these tools for my own use. They connect services
I use and automate tasks on my personal devices and server.
They are not offered as a public service.

## Dreeve sync

Dreeve sync uses rclone to download workout files exported
by Health Sync to my Google Drive. The files are imported
into my self-hosted Dreeve instance for training analysis.

It requests read-only Google Drive access. The configured
workflow downloads workout files and does not modify or
delete files in Google Drive.
