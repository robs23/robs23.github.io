# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy covers the personal tools listed on the
[homepage](index.html). These tools are operated by me
for my own use and are not offered as a public service.

## Dreeve sync

**Access:** Dreeve sync requests read-only access to Google
Drive file metadata and contents. Its configured workflow
reads the workout export folder and downloads workout files.

**Purpose:** Downloaded files are used for personal training
analysis in my self-hosted Dreeve instance.

**Storage:** Workout files and imported activity data are
stored on my personal server. OAuth credentials are stored
in the server's rclone configuration. Credential backups
may be stored in my password manager.

**Sharing:** The tool does not sell Google user data, use it
for advertising, or intentionally share workout files with
other people. Google Drive provides the source storage;
my personal server processes the downloaded files.

**Retention:** Downloaded archives and imported training data
remain until I delete them. Credentials remain until I
remove them or revoke the tool's authorization.

## Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked through
[Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

Revoking access prevents further authorized access but does
not delete previously downloaded files. Those files and
imported activity data must be deleted separately.

## Future tools and policy changes

Additional tools will be listed here with their specific
data access, purpose, storage, sharing, and retention details
before they are used.
